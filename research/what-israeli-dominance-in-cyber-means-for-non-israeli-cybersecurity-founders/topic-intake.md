## Why Now?

Wiz ($32B to Google), CyberArk ($25B to Palo Alto), and Armis ($7.75B to ServiceNow) closed or neared completion in 2025–2026, reinforcing Israel's cyber exit flywheel ([Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcy5ft711e)).

## Initial Hypothesis

Israel's advantage is ecosystem density (founder mutual support, hands-on cyber VCs, military talent pipeline), but AI commoditization and GTM saturation are eroding playbook advantages—large independent US vendors remain category leaders.

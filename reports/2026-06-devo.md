## Executive Summary

Devo Technology is a **late-stage private SIEM/data platform vendor** (Boston) selling a cloud-native **Security Data Platform** with bundled SIEM, SOAR, and UEBA. Its last disclosed funding is **Series F $100M at $2B valuation (June 2022)** with total raised **>$500M** ([CRN](https://www.crn.com/news/security/devo-raises-another-100m-to-grow-sales-operations)). The "autonomous SOC" narrative builds on the **Kognos acquisition (2022)** and **DeepTrace** AI investigation features—tier A/B evidence supports **analyst augmentation**, not a fully autonomous alertless SOC. **Ken Naumann** became CEO in March 2025 ([Markets Insider](https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/devo-technology-appoints-ken-naumann-as-ceo-1034444521)). Differentiation vs Splunk centers on **streaming no-index ingestion**, high-volume query performance, and integrated SIEM/SOAR/UEBA ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/2022/06/02/devo-technology-attack-surface/)).

## Landscape Context

### The problem

Legacy SIEMs struggle with data volume, cost, and analyst workload. Devo targets high-throughput log analytics with AI-assisted hunting layered on top.

### Incumbents

Splunk Enterprise Security, Microsoft Sentinel, Elastic Security, and autonomous SOC startups (Dropzone AI, Prophet Security).

## What It Is

Per [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/2022/06/02/devo-technology-attack-surface/) and [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2022/06/02/devo-raises-100m-2b-valuation-ai-powered-cybersecurity-platform/):

- Cloud-native platform ingesting logs, metrics, traces at **hundreds of TB/day**
- **HyperStream** ingestion; parallel query engines; **400-day hot storage** (vendor claims)
- **SIEM + SOAR + UEBA** in one platform
- **DeepTrace** for autonomous hunting/investigation; **Collective Defense** community intel
- **FedRAMP Moderate ATO** (Dec 2023, separate environment per [TOMs PDF](https://www.devo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Devo-Security-Data-Platform-TOMs.pdf))

## Differentiation Analysis

| Dimension | Devo | Splunk ES | Microsoft Sentinel |
|-----------|------|-----------|-------------------|
| Architecture | Streaming SIEM/data platform | Mature SPL ecosystem | Azure-native KQL |
| AI/SOC automation | DeepTrace + Kognos lineage | SOAR + ML features | Copilot, Logic Apps SOAR |
| Openness | Proprietary SaaS | Hybrid | Azure-tied |
| Pricing | Platform bundle narrative | Per-GB + apps | Consumption on Azure |

### Novel vs incremental

**Incremental:** AI investigation features overlap incumbent ML/copilot roadmaps. **Differentiated:** high-volume streaming ingestion architecture and MSSP multi-tenant positioning (SiliconANGLE).

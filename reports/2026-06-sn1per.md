## Executive Summary

**Sn1per** is an **automated penetration testing and attack surface management (ASM) orchestration platform** by 1N3 that chains **90+ security tools** (Nessus, Burp, Metasploit, Nuclei, etc.) into unified recon-scan-exploit-report workflows ([README](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/blob/master/README.md)). With **~10,245 GitHub stars** and active commits through June 2026, it targets offensive security and continuous ASM—not defensive SOC operations. Community Edition uses a **restrictive custom EULA** (not OSI-open); Pro adds Web UI and commercial support. Differentiation vs **Nessus/Burp** is **workflow orchestration breadth**, not scanner engine depth.

## Landscape Context

### The problem

Pentesters and ASM teams juggle dozens of CLI tools; Sn1per provides pre-wired pipelines and workspace/loot management.

### Incumbents

Tenable Nessus, Burp Suite, Qualys, Cobalt Strike (different segment), commercial ASM (Censys, Palo Alto Cortex Xpanse).

## What It Is

Per [Sn1per README](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/blob/master/README.md):

- Scan modes: `normal`, `stealth`, `discover`, `webscan`, `vulnscan`, mass modes
- **600+ exploits**, **10,000+ detections** (SC0PE framework, vendor claim)
- Integrations: [Nessus](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/wiki/Nessus-Integration), [Burp Pro](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/wiki/Burpsuite-Professional-2.x-Integration)
- Editions: Community (CLI), Professional (~$984/yr/seat per README), Enterprise

## Key Findings

- Sn1per orchestrates **90+ tools** into ASM and pentest workflows ([README](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/blob/master/README.md)).
- **~10.2k GitHub stars** and 2026 activity indicate sustained community adoption ([GitHub](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per)).
- **Integrates Nessus and Burp** rather than replacing them ([Nessus wiki](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/wiki/Nessus-Integration)).
- Community Edition **EULA prohibits derivative products**—inspectable but not freely forkable for commercial products ([LICENSE.md](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/blob/master/LICENSE.md)).
- Value is **pipeline automation**; core detection logic is repackaged from underlying tools ([README](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/blob/master/README.md)).
